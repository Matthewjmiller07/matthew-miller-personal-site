The market

Since 2015 Israeli food chains must publish every price in every branch, every day. A handful of services turn those files into comparisons, and the savings are real: a 2026 survey of 178 products found a ₪443 (17.7%) gap between the cheapest and dearest chain, and a Rosh Hashana basket of 150 products differed by ₪577.1,2 Yet the same reporting finds that most Israelis keep shopping at the same chain, and that use of the comparison apps is far below their potential.1,3 The data exists. The habit does not.

What is in the box

  • Search in English or Hebrew, by name, brand or barcode, across every branch at once.
  • Barcode scan with the phone camera (or from a photo). Pick up a product in one shop and see its price in the other six.
  • Smart list. Prices the whole list at every branch, applies "2 for ₪X" and "1+1" offers, optional club prices, and suggests a two-stop plan when splitting the shop saves more.
  • Shopping mode to tick items off in the aisle.
  • Receipt audit, read on the device in a few seconds.
  • WhatsApp, share sheet and copy export, with a link that carries the list.
  • Insights page with five charts, each with a table view, in light and dark mode.
  • Installable and offline. Home-screen icon, shortcuts to Scan, List and Receipt.
  • Product photos and English names from Open Food Facts where it has them.

How it works

A scheduled job reads each chain's official store list, keeps the branches whose city is Ra'anana, downloads that day's full price and promotion files, and publishes four small data files. The app is static: no database, no login, no running cost. Every time a price differs from the previous run the change is appended to a history file, so the price-history charts fill in on their own. New chains are one entry in a list of sources.

Honest limits today
  • One chain. Shufersal publishes openly. Rami Levy, Yochananof and others publish through a shared portal that requires signing in with each chain's public account, and Carrefour and Victory did not answer from our build servers. Until they are added, CHP is the better tool for chain-versus-chain questions.
  • Receipt audit relies on barcodes printed on the receipt and has been tested on clean images, not yet on crumpled thermal paper.
  • Offers are simplified to sale price, "N for ₪X" and "1+1". Coupons and basket-level deals are left out.
