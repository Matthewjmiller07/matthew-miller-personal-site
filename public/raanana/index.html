tiny ra'anana רעננה הקטנה

All of Ra'anana as one isometric pixel-art map, drawn from Google's 3D city model. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Search for any street or address below.

How it was made
  1. One orthographic camera, tilted 45° and turned 15°, renders the city from Google's 3D tiles in 26 shots that line up to the pixel. 1 px = 0.586 m.
  2. Nano Banana Pro redrew 38 render frames as pixel art; checks against the render, and a look by eye, kept 30.
  3. Qwen-Image-Edit redrew all 127 overlapping windows. Each overlap is cut along the path where neighbours agree best, so no window edge shows.
  4. Total cloud spend: about $14.

Imagery derived from Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles (Map data ©2026 Google). City limits, streets, addresses and places © OpenStreetMap contributors. Inspired by tiny.london and isometric.nyc.
