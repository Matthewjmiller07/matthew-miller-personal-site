## 🏆 Headliner: Hineni: The Tefillin Trials

A 3D game for our chabura on Hilchot Tefillin, siman 25: one bracha or two, and what counts as a hefsek. You walk around a shul with your shel yad on, and the gabbai has your shel rosh.

- **The Hefsek Gauntlet.** Shmerel, your kid and the Rav chase you to get you talking, and people throw phones at you (Space sends out a Do Not Disturb pulse). When Kaddish starts, a giant AMEN button dares you to press it.
- **Bracha Catch.** לְהָנִיחַ and עַל מִצְוַת balls bounce out of the Aron, and you catch only the ones Rashi or Rabbeinu Tam needs.
- **Hum or Hush.** Hum a niggun while the guests wash (Rav Chaim did), and stop at Hamotzi.

Twenty poskim wander the shul. Walk up to one and he tells you his ruling. Since launch it has had a glow-up: stained glass with light shafts, a gilded Aron, and chandeliers that really light the room. The bloom effect kept blacking out the screen, and the cause was one NaN pixel.

[Play it →](https://theothermatthewmiller.com/tefillin-trials)

## 🖍️ New: Color by Number, from any photo

Drop in any photo and get a sheet to color, using only the markers you own: a 40-marker box, the classic 10, dot dabbers, crayons. The page cuts the photo into a few thousand regions whose edges follow the picture, matches each to your closest marker, and merges anything too small to hold a number. Or switch to Dots. It can also blank out the background so you only color the subject.

Print the sheet with or without the answer page, color it on screen, or make a GIF that colors it in one color at a time. It started as an app with a server, logins and a paywall. Now it's one page, and nothing gets uploaded.

[Make a sheet →](https://theothermatthewmiller.com/color-by-number)

## 🍪 Which Bracha?

Type any food, or a whole Kiddush table, and get the bracha before and after in Hebrew, the order to say them in, and how the after-brachot combine into one Al Hamichya. Jev doesn't rule: it answers four factual questions per food (mainly made of what? still recognizable? real grain in it? one of the seven species?), and the rules of brachot, written in code, decide. Because Jev gives probabilities, the page flags close calls. **Pringles are 59% grain food.** Potato kugel is a coin flip on whether its flour counts, so the page shows the other bracha too.

[Try your Kiddush →](https://theothermatthewmiller.com/bracha)
