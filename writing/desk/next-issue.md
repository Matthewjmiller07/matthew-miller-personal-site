Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- Color by Number (/color-by-number): drop in any photo and get a sheet to color, using only the markers you own (a 40-marker box, the classic 10, dot dabbers, crayons, or any colors). It cuts the photo into a few thousand SLIC superpixels whose edges follow the picture, snaps each to your closest marker in CIELAB, and merges anything too small to hold a number. Or switch to Dots for a dot-marker grid. An optional in-browser segmentation model (MODNet) blanks out the background so just the subject is left to color. You can color it on screen, or print it. It started life as a Replit app with a server, logins and a paywall; now it's one page and nothing gets uploaded. Print the plain black-and-white sheet, the sheet with the answer on a second page, or just the answer, or make a GIF that colors the sheet in, color by color, and lands on the finished picture.
- Place names, by the book (/israel?tab=names): 45 facts about Israeli place names from the Academy of the Hebrew Language's settlements quiz and its official names list. It's Kfar SaVA (סָבָא is Aramaic for 'grandfather', stressed on the last syllable, and Josephus spelled it with a single beta), Hefa with a tsere (Haifa is the Arabic), Natsrat, Ma'ale Adummim (nothing to do with Edom), and the moshav next to Ra'anana is officially Botsra. Kiryat Mal'akhi is a tribute to Los Angeles, Rehovot is one of Isaac's wells, and Ra'anana's official Latin spelling has a double n.
- Which Bracha? (/bracha): type any food, or a whole Kiddush table, and get the bracha before and after in Hebrew, plus the order to say them in and how the after-brachot combine into one Al Hamichya. Jev never rules: it answers four factual questions per food (mainly made of what? produce still recognizable? real grain in it? one of the seven species?) and the rules of brachot, in code, decide. Because Jev gives probabilities, the page flags close calls: Pringles is 59% grain food, and potato kugel is a coin flip on whether its flour counts, so it shows the other bracha too.
- Parsha Ties (doppelgifter.com/parsha-ties/, listed on /projects): a necktie for each of the 54 parshiot. FLUX.2 [klein] 4B draws each parsha as a repeating pattern of its own symbols, which gets tiled into a print-ready tie file, and Printify turns that into a real product with mockups. The page finds this week's parsha for you. Price joke: code YUD takes 10% off because yud = 10 in gematria.
- The Podcast of Jewish Ideas vs. the Thirteen Principles (/jj): every three-minute section of J.J. Kimche's Torah in Motion podcast, rated by Jev against each of the Rambam's thirteen principles on a 0–4 scale (not relevant → open denial), drawn as a heat strip per episode with the flagged moments, a timestamp and the sentence Jev picked as the evidence. Torah min hashamayim (#8) and the Torah's permanence (#9) light up far more than anything about God; that's what an academic Bible-and-history podcast sounds like. Jev also guesses whether a speaker endorses an idea or is just explaining Spinoza, and the page is upfront that it often can't tell. Episodes 76–93 had transcripts in the RSS feed; the other 82 are going through whisper.cpp on a Mac mini and fill in as they finish.
- Hineni: The Tefillin Trials (/tefillin-trials): a 3D game for the SCP chabura on Hilchot Tefillin, siman 25 (one bracha or two, and what counts as a hefsek). You walk around a shul. In the Hefsek Gauntlet your shel yad is on and the gabbai has your shel rosh. Shmerel, your kid and the Rav chase you to get you talking, phones get flung at you (Space blasts them away with a Do Not Disturb pulse), and when Kaddish starts a giant AMEN button dares you to press it. In Bracha Catch, לְהָנִיחַ and עַל מִצְוַת balls bounce out of the Aron and you catch only what Rashi or Rabbeinu Tam needs; for Rabbeinu Tam's tefillin you dodge them all. In Hum or Hush you hum a niggun while the guests wash (Rav Chaim did), then stop at Hamotzi. Twenty poskim wander the shul, and if you walk up to one he tells you his ruling. It opens on the shiur's three Hinenis. Built with React Three Fiber, drei, Rapier physics, Yuka steering AI and three.quarks particles. Bloom kept blacking out the screen, and it turned out one NaN pixel was enough; the fix was a scrub pass before the bloom.
- Coffee deep links (/coffee?topic=...): the coffee page now takes a topic in the URL and jumps straight to that question, opened and highlighted, e.g. /coffee?topic=Brachot%20%E2%80%94%20Bracha%20Acharona (a partial slug like ?topic=bracha-acharona works too). Handy for linking someone to the exact halacha in a message or the newsletter.
- Vayera parasha sheet (/parasha-sheet/vayera): the Mega Bible's one-sheet study page for Genesis 18–22, counted from the morphology. Thirteen words occur nowhere else in the Bible, and nine are in chapter 22: eight are names in the sixty-word report of Nahor's family, and the ninth is וַיַּעֲקֹד, the verb that gave the Binding its name. Inside the Binding, God's names split five and five: אֱלֹהִים tests and commands, and the Name arrives with the angel's call. The Modern Hebrew notes come from Ma'agarim and Balashon: the overthrow of Sodom (מהפכה) became a revolution, Ishmael the archer (רֹבֶה קשת) became the rifle, and a test (נסה) became an attempt. Uriel Simon on Abraham's two answers, Sodom and Moriah, is at the bottom.
- Tefillin Trials glow-up (/tefillin-trials): stained-glass windows with light shafts raking across the shul, a gilded Aron with columns and a spotlight, chandeliers that really light the room, depth-of-field on the title screen, SMAA and colour grading, glass-panel HUD with a foil-gold logo, and shul-goers with eyes, brows, collars and shoes.
