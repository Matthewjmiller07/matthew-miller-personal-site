Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- Lost in Translation (/lost-in-translation): a Hebrew mistranslation guessing game. Live cards pick a random verse from Sefaria, run single words through a machine translator with no context, and a judge model picks a genuine misreading, checked against Sefaria's English before the card is served.
- Rav Asher Weiss quote cards: 3 more on 11 October (19 in all).
- The Book of Generations (/genealogies): every name in the genealogies of Bereshit and Noach, Adam to Abram and the seventy nations, as a radial family tree and a lifespan timeline you can scrub by year from Creation, cross-referenced with 75 years of Israeli baby names from the Central Bureau of Statistics (filter by years, sex, and Jewish, Muslim, Christian Arab or Druze). Opening a name pulls its Wikipedia summary and the notable Israelis who carry it from Wikidata, live. Comes with a 64-second trailer (16:9 and vertical). Best findings: Jubal is the most-given name in these chapters (40,599 Yuvals); Adam tied with Amir as the top Druze boys' name in 2000 and in 2024 was given to 593 Jewish, 664 Muslim and 42 Druze babies; Iscah, mentioned once in the Torah, hit a record 220 girls in 2024, while Nimrod peaked at 128 boys in 1983 and fell to 5.
- Mega Bible now has a Genealogies layer (/mega-bible?book=Genesis&chapter=5): on Genesis 4–5 and 10–11 a card links to The Book of Generations, and every name in the verses is underlined with its lifespan and how many Israeli babies carry it (hover Methuselah: 969 years, 5 babies).
