Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- **Reckon** (`/reckon/`): a grocery price app for Ra'anana that installs from the browser. It reads the price files Shufersal must publish by law for its seven Ra'anana branches (13,000 products), lets you scan a barcode or photograph a receipt, prices your whole list at every branch and sends it to WhatsApp. Interesting because the same chain charges 12% more at its Express branches than at its Deal branches a few streets away, and a bag of Bamba runs from ₪4.90 to ₪6.90. The two-page case for it is at `/reckon/why/`. (Held out of the 16 November issue; leave out the Bamba line if it runs.)
- **The Atlas** (`/atlas`): the whole site as a night sky. Every public page is a star (203 at launch), sorted into nine constellations, with the daf games as a spiral in the middle in the order of the daily cycle. Drag, zoom, search, or hit "Surprise me" to be flown to a random page. Interesting because the chart draws itself from the pages at build time, so each new daf game or tool shows up as a new star without anyone adding it; pages from the last three weeks ripple. A star can be linked directly, e.g. `/atlas#/mega-bible`. Also new on the homepage and in the nav.

- Homepage polish: the hero diorama now rotates from a drag anywhere in the header, and the random-facts ticker on `/` scrolls at a calm, readable pace.
- **The Produce Aisle** (`/produce`): an Israeli supermarket's fruit-and-vegetable section in 3D. Scroll to walk down the aisle, click any of 32 crates, and its Hebrew name opens: the verses (straight into the Mega Bible), the rabbinic sources (Sefaria), where the word comes from, and links to Ma'agarim, the Academy and Balashon. Interesting because the aisle is organized by the texts: the Seven Species, a section stocked entirely from Numbers 11:5 (cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, garlic), Abaye's Rosh Hashanah simanim, and the words modern Hebrew had to invent. Modern Hebrew gave the cucumber the Mishnah's melon name and the zucchini the Bible's cucumber name, and the banana turned down מוז. Click a crate twice (or hit "Pick one up") and a piece lifts out of the crate into your hand, in high detail, to turn over and look at up close. Also a new tab on `/israel` (`?tab=produce`). New: every crate now says how often its word occurs in the Tanakh, and six are hapax legomena, found exactly once: four of them in a single verse, Numbers 11:5, where the cucumbers, melons, onions and garlic of Egypt are named and never again (plus the wild gourds of 2 Kings 4:39 and the nut garden of Song of Songs 6:11). Those crates carry a black ✦ HAPAX flash on their price tags.
