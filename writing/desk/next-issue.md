Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- **WarmHouse** (`/warmhouse`): a new brand page for custom 3D-printed models of homes, made as closing gifts for real-estate agents. You can spin four house models in the browser, see your name on the brass base plate, and pick one to order. Interesting because the 3D viewer is drawing the same four-colour files the printer uses.
- **WarmHouse turntable GIFs** (`/warmhouse/gifs/`): each realtor's modeled listing now renders as a looping GIF of the house turning on its brass name plate, filmed from the same in-browser 3D viewer the site uses. Made for outreach emails, where a moving picture of someone's own listing does the pitching.
- **Kama Ze** (`/kamaze/`): a grocery price app for Ra'anana that installs from the browser. It reads the price files Shufersal must publish by law for its seven Ra'anana branches (13,000 products), lets you scan a barcode or photograph a receipt, prices your whole list at every branch and sends it to WhatsApp. Interesting because the same chain charges 12% more at its Express branches than at its Deal branches a few streets away, and a bag of Bamba runs from ₪4.90 to ₪6.90. The two-page case for it is at `/kamaze/why/`.
