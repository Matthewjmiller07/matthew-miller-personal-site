Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- Mega Bible word lookups (/mega-bible?book=Genesis&chapter=1&cmp=1254,776&concx=1): click any word and you can now show only the verses in the passage with any form of its root (or just that spelling), list every verse in the Tanakh with the same Strong's number, grouped by book and filterable by spelling, and "+ Compare" up to six roots, each marked in its own colour, staying marked as you move from chapter to chapter. With two or more compared, "Together in Tanakh" lists every verse where they all appear (בָּרָא and אֶרֶץ together: 15 verses, from Genesis 1:1 to Isaiah 65:17). Every lookup is in the URL, so a link opens straight onto it.
- The tefillin card off the 3D printer (/3d-print-tools/leshem-yichud#make): the same artwork as true-size SVGs for a laser cutter or vinyl cutter, with ideas sized to a public-library makerspace's machines (Skokie's Studio: Epilog and Glowforge lasers, Cricuts, a Silhouette, two embroidery machines, a heat press). Laser-engraved maple, clear acrylic engraved from behind so the front stays glossy, a leather card for the tefillin bag, a slate plaque, gold iron-on vinyl for a tallis bag, and a one-colour STL that fits the library's free two-hour 3D print. Each idea has a preview rendered from the real card geometry onto wood, acrylic, leather, slate, vinyl, felt and PLA textures.
- The Likutei Peshatim community calendar (/calendars) is current again: it had quietly stopped after Ekev in July, and twelve weeks of HTC's newsletter (119 shiurim, open houses, Yamim Noraim and Sukkos events, and yahrzeits) are now back in the feed. It now refreshes itself in the cloud every Thursday night, and raises an alarm if it ever falls more than two weeks behind.
