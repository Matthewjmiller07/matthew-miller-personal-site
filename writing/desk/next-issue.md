Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- Mega Bible music video: "Four Hundred Men" (`/mega-bible?book=Genesis&chapter=32`, also on Genesis 33) — Vayishlach as a Broadway-style musical: Jacob and Esau get ready to meet, with lip-synced verses for each brother and a split-screen duet at the end. Song made in Suno, shots in Hugging Face and Higgsfield, with both brothers locked to one face each across every shot.
- Aptonym certificates (`/aptonyms/certificate`): if your name is your job, claim a "Certified Aptonym" credential from The Aptonym Registry. Every claim is reviewed by hand; an issued one gets its own verification page (`/aptonyms/certificate/APT-…`), a one-click "Add to LinkedIn profile" button that files it under Licenses & certifications with a link back to the site, a share post, and a downloadable certificate image. Every aptonym's page now has an "Are you this person?" link to claim theirs, and everyone already in the registry can be sent a personal link that issues their certificate instantly. Nominative determinism, now with paperwork.
- The Mighty Hand (`/yad`): all 15,133 halakhot of the Mishneh Torah, read one at a time by TypeSafe's Jev model, which asked nine questions of each (what it does, what it's about, who it's for, stakes, warmth, abstraction, can it be kept today, does it turn on a number, does it give a reason), and then picked a gem from each of the 1,006 chapters. The answers are drawn as art: a 14-petal rose with one ray per chapter, a seismograph of warmth against stakes, a loom of every halakha, a constellation of the 84 sections, and a painting for each book. Headline: 60% of it can be kept today (the Book of Love is 98% live, Service 1%). Also, money is warmer than the altar, and of 1,006 chapters Jev found exactly one of joy. Cost of reading the whole thing: $1.46.
- The Mighty Hand, part two (`/yad`, "Does it change in Israel?"): a reader asked whether what still applies changes between the Land of Israel and the diaspora, so Jev reread all 15,133 halakhot with two new questions. 68% can be kept in Israel today and 61% outside it. Thirteen of the fourteen books barely move; the whole gap is the Book of Seeds (tithes, terumah, shemitta, gifts to the poor), which is 81% live in Israel and 18% outside it. Cost: $0.47.
- Gratitude, a Thanksgiving source sheet (`/thanksgiving`, full text at `/sourcesheets/gratitude`): 35 sources in six movements, from Rashi's reading of Babel as Adam's inherited ingratitude, to Leah as the first person ever to thank God (from inside a heartbreak), to Rav Kook's "feed the animals first" and a rabbi thanking the Pilgrims for choosing a kosher bird. Comes with a 13-page printable PDF, a 36-second film (Higgsfield footage, HyperFrames titles), and a vertical short on the Hebrew root י-ד-ה, where thank you, I admit and Yehudah turn out to be the same word.
