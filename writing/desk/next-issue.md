Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- Color by Number (/color-by-number): drop in any photo and get a sheet to color, using only the markers you own (a 40-marker box, the classic 10, dot dabbers, crayons, or any colors). It cuts the photo into a few thousand SLIC superpixels whose edges follow the picture, snaps each to your closest marker in CIELAB, and merges anything too small to hold a number. Or switch to Dots for a dot-marker grid. An optional in-browser segmentation model (MODNet) blanks out the background so just the subject is left to color. You can color it on screen, or print it. It started life as a Replit app with a server, logins and a paywall; now it's one page and nothing gets uploaded.
