Running list for the next Workshop. Add builds, links and angles as they happen.

- **Ki: Coverdale's "yee" is "yea"** (`/mega-bible/ki/?ref=Genesis+3:1`). The 1535 Coverdale Bible never writes "yea"; it spells it "yee", the same spelling the King James uses for "you". The ki analysis now reads spelling per translation (plus Wycliffe's "yhe" and "indede"/"in deede"), which finds a word in 49 more places. Genesis 3:1 is the nice case: Coverdale splits אַף כִּי into "Yee, hath God sayde indede".
