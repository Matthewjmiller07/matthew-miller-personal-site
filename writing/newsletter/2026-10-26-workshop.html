🏆 Headliner

All of Shas, mapped

Open any daf in the Mega Talmud and press “Map the argument with Jev.” Every segment gets labelled with the move it makes: Mishnah, question, answer, derivation from a verse, baraita, dispute, ruling, story or explanation. You get a coloured strip of the whole daf, an outline of its sugyot (“Question ×2 → Answer → From a verse → …”), and a filter that picks out every question or every baraita on the page. On Berakhot 2, the Mishnah is dispute → story → verse, and the Gemara is one long chain of 15 questions and 10 answers.

Then I ran it on everything. Jev has now read every segment of the Bavli: 81,709 of them across 2,684 dafim, for about four dollars. Every daf opens with its map already drawn, and the Flow section draws any masechet, any seder, or all of Shas as one woven tapestry. Pick a palette (including a colour-blind-safe one) or recolour any move, and download it as a PNG.

What shows up: questions and answers make up more than half of the Gemara, the red-and-green weave. Purple Mishnah bands mark each new perek, and the gaps are where new sugyot begin.

And the wall version: Shas as art. Every masechet, every seder and all of Shas as an 18×24 poster in three colourways, with framed mockups.

🎨 New

The Painted Torah

Every chapter of the Torah as a painting: 187 of them, with no prompt written by hand. For each chapter Jev reads every verse and chooses the moment to paint. Genesis 3 gets the cherubim and the fiery sword at Eden's gate, Genesis 1 the great sea monsters. Then it art-directs the moment: setting, light, weather, framing, palette, painting tradition, and scores for tension and scale. Code turns the answers into a prompt and FLUX.2 [klein] paints it. All the art direction cost three cents of Jev.

  • Filter for the 18 night scenes, or sort by tension. Numbers 16, Korah's rebellion, tops it.
  • The image model fights back: it letters whole verses into the picture, so speech is stripped and Tesseract repaints anything with stray writing. Any word for God made it paint a figure in the sky, so divine names are cut from the prompt entirely.
  • Every painting is filed under its parsha. See each week's on the Parasha Hub, and find the chapter's painting at the top of every Torah chapter in the Mega Bible.

👩 Mega Bible

The Bechdel test for Tanakh

Does a chapter have two named women who talk to each other about something other than a man? Jev answered all three questions for every chapter of Tanakh, for about a nickel. Very few pass:

  • Passes: Rachel and Leah over the mandrakes (Genesis 30), Naomi and Ruth on the road (Ruth 1–3).
  • Doesn't: Deborah and Yael never meet (Judges 4), Sarai and Hagar never speak to each other (Genesis 16), and Miriam isn't named in Exodus 2.
  • Switch on the strict version (mostly not about a man) and Ruth 3, spent planning for Boaz, drops out.

🕍 Mega Siddur

Is Koren really the only siddur with the right order?

Rabbi Elyada said only Koren gets the order of the morning blessings right, with no verses like רֵאשִׁית חָכְמָה before Birkat HaTorah. I traced it from Seder Rav Amram to ArtScroll through the scans themselves, and the story flips. The “correct” order is the Tur's personal 14th-century reform (“it was difficult for me, saying verses before Birkat HaTorah”). The Gra defended the older order, and Machzor Vitry already opened with מה טבו around 1100. And ראשית חכמה isn't in any of the classic siddurim checked, including Seder HaYom, where Modeh Ani was born.
