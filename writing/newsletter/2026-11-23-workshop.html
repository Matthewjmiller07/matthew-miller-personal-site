🍇 Headliner

The Produce Aisle: every crate has a verse

ClaudeSand3D models

The Produce Aisle is an Israeli supermarket's fruit-and-vegetable section in 3D. Scroll to walk, click any of 32 crates, and its Hebrew name opens: the verses, the rabbinic sources, where the word comes from.

The aisle is organized by the texts: the Seven Species, Abaye's Rosh Hashanah simanim, and a whole section stocked from Numbers 11:5, where the cucumbers, melons, onions and garlic of Egypt are named once and never again. Six crates are hapax legomena and wear a black ✦ HAPAX tag. Along the way: modern Hebrew gave the cucumber the Mishnah's melon name and the zucchini the Bible's cucumber name.

Click a crate twice and a piece lifts out into your hand to turn over up close.

☠️ Not for the faint of stomach

The Slop Kitchen: a cookbook for food that should never exist

ClaudeTypeSafe JevFLUX.2 [klein]

TypeSafe's Jev model tasted 80 random food combinations, scored how revolting each one is, and threw out the ones that were actually unsafe (only the toothpaste-gum ones). Claude wrote straight-faced, magazine-style recipes for the twelve worst, and FLUX.2 [klein] shot the food photos.

The joke is that three different models, a judge, a writer and a photographer, all take it completely seriously. Every dish has its own link and a button that sends the recipe card to WhatsApp, for the friend who deserves it.
